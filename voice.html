🎙️ Audition voice remote

Connecting to the sidebar…
Mic level shows here while listening.
Wear headphones so the mic doesn’t pick up the auditions. Keep this window open and visible.
📝 Listening for your note…
Say “next”, “good”, “peak”, “custom 3.5”, “N/A” or “notes …”
Commands
next1, open next
good3 + yellow, open next
peak5 + green, open next
custom …“custom three point five” (or say “custom”, pause, then the number). Just saying a number works too. Below 3 no colour, 3+ yellow, 4+ green
N/AListened = No, both ratings N/A, red, open next
notes …Add a note. Or say “notes”, pause, then the note.
descendingWork top → bottom
ascendingWork bottom → top
top / bottomJump to the first open audition from that end
backPrevious audition, rating unchanged
undoRevert the last rating
replay / pause / playAudio controls
